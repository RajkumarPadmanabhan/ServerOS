How to use ServerOS

  1. Leave ServerOS running on the device that should keep the files. You may close the browser tab. Do not close the ServerOS window.
  2. On your phone, tablet, or another computer, open the address shown in ServerOS.
  3. Log in with your account. Another person can create their own login and still open the owner's files. Their files stay separate.
  4. Upload files from any of those devices. They are saved on the device you picked.
  5. Live files open only while the device that has them is on. A backup that was copied to another device can still be opened when the original is off.
  6. To keep files on a second computer too, install ServerOS there and choose “Join a ServerOS I already started”.
  7. Old phones and tablets do not need an app store. Open the address in the browser, then use Add to Home Screen.
  8. An Android phone can be the backup device if Node.js is installed in Termux. It asks Android for storage permission first. An iPhone can open and upload files, but it cannot stay on as the server.

Your password stops other people on the network from opening the files. The files themselves sit on your device. Use ServerOS on a network you trust.

If you forget the password, use the recovery key you saved. On the main device you can also reset the password from the login screen.
